Updates and corrections

The corrections log for SMSF Property Investing. Superannuation rules keep moving, so anything that changes or turns out to be wrong after publication gets recorded here, plainly and permanently.

2027 edition

July 2026. The 2027 edition published, fully rewritten for the new rules: the residential borrowing ban commencing 10 August 2026, Division 296, and the May 2026 budget changes. It replaces the first edition and its July 2026 reform update.

Title update. The book is now titled SMSF Property Investing, and is available on Amazon in Kindle and paperback alongside the free web edition here. The text is unchanged.

No corrections have been logged for this edition yet.

How corrections work

When a rule changes or an error is found, the reading version at /super/ is corrected first and the change is logged on this page with a date. The PDF and EPUB downloads are regenerated with each new edition rather than patched in place, so the log is the source of truth between editions.

Spotted something wrong? Tell me via LinkedIn and I will check it, credit you if you want, and log the fix here.
